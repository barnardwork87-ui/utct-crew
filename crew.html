Rules of engagement

Three rules.Learn them.

01

Within 100 m only

Help him anywhere other than a crew station and he gets a 30-minute penalty. That includes “just a quick sip” on the side of the road. Josh will be enforcing this.

02

He does not sit down

Chairs are for the crew. The one exception is Hout Bay, where he gets 5 minutes in a chair while his socks get changed. Davey ends the sitting.

03

One voice at a time

Nine people shouting “you’re looking strong” at once sounds like an ambush. Mikey L talks food, Josh talks time, Matty talks to his head. Everyone else cheers from a respectful distance.
